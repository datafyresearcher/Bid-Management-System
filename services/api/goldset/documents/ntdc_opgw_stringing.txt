NATIONAL TRANSMISSION & DISPATCH COMPANY LIMITED (NTDC)
INVITATION FOR BIDS

IFB NO: NTDC/220KV/2026/0912
TITLE: Procurement of OPGW, hardware and stringing of 220kV D/C transmission
line from 500kV Grid Station Lahore to 220/132kV Substation Khanpur.

1. SCOPE OF WORK
The scope includes supply of 48 fibre OPGW, tower hardware, spacer dampers,
insulators, stringing of one 220kV double circuit line of approximately 46 km
complete with all testing and commissioning.

2. TIME SCHEDULE
The successful Bidder shall complete the works within 300 days from the date
of Letter of Award.

3. BID SECURITY AND TENDER FEE

3.1 Bid security shall be 2% of the Contract Price. Where the Contract Price
is not quoted, bid security shall be Rs. 4,000,000 (Rupees Four Million only)
in the form of a Bank Guarantee.

3.2 The tender fee is Rs. 15,000 (Rupees Fifteen Thousand only) payable in cash
or by pay order before collection of the bidding document.

3.3 The last date for receipt of bids is 25th November 2026 at 1000 hours.

3.4 Bids shall be opened on 26th November 2026 at 1100 hours at NTDC Head
Office, WAPDA House, Lahore.

4. ELIGIBILITY
4.1 The Contractor shall hold a valid Pakistan Engineering Council licence in
category C-A.
4.2 The Contractor shall have completed not less than 4 similar works of
transmission line nature during the last 10 years.
4.3 The Contractor shall have a minimum average annual turnover of
Rs. 1,800,000,000.
4.4 The Contractor shall furnish an affidavit of non-blacklisting and evidence
of registration with the Sales Tax and Income Tax departments.
4.5 The Contractor shall submit a Power of Attorney, Form of Tender and the
Schedule of Prices, duly completed and signed.

5. CONDITIONS
5.1 The Contract shall be on a firm price, fixed price without price
adjustment basis.
5.2 The Contractor shall be liable for unlimited liability in respect of any
defect in the works.
5.3 Liquidated damages at 0.05 percent of the Contract Price per day shall
apply for delay, subject to an overall cap of 5 percent.
5.4 Ten percent retention money shall be deducted from each running bill.
5.5 The Employer reserves the right to terminate for convenience.
5.6 The Employer may grant an extension of time on account of force majeure.
5.7 All disputes shall be referred to arbitration in Singapore under ICC rules.
5.8 Foreign arbitration seat and jurisdiction clauses are not excluded.
5.9 The Contractor shall arrange third party liability insurance.
5.10 The Contractor shall be responsible for any local employment obligations.

6. NOTES
Prospective bidders are advised to attend the pre-bid meeting on 12-Nov-2026
at 1000 hours. Queries shall be submitted in writing by 08-Nov-2026.
