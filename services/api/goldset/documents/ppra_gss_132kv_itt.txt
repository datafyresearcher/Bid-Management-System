PUNJAB PUBLIC PROCUREMENT REGULATORY AUTHORITY
TENDER NOTICE — GOODS / WORKS

TENDER NO: PPRA/PH/2026/4487
INVITING ENTITY: Lesco (Lahore Electric Supply Company) Ltd.
CATEGORY: Substations
DURATION: 9 months
ESTIMATED COST: Rs. 187,500,000

DESCRIPTION: Construction of new 132kV Grid Station including extension of
existing 132/11kV substation, installation of 1x63MVA power transformer, bay
extension, control house and associated civil works at Sundar Industrial Estate,
Lahore.

1. INSTRUCTIONS TO BIDDERS

1.1 The bidding documents may be obtained from the office of the Chief
Procurement & Contracts Manager, Lesco Head Office, Wapda House, Lahore, on
payment of a tender fee of Rs. 25,000 (Twenty Five Thousand Rupees only),
non-refundable, through pay order / demand draft in favour of Lesco.

1.2 Bid security of Rs. 5,000,000 (Rupees Five Million only) shall be
submitted along with the bid in the form of a Bank Guarantee issued by a
schedule bank acceptable to the Employer. The bid security shall remain valid
for 120 days from the date of opening of bids.

1.3 The last date for submission of bids is 25-Nov-2026 at 11:00 hours. Bids
received after this time will be opened in the presence of the bidders'
representatives and shall not be entertained.

1.4 The bids shall be opened on 26-Nov-2026 at 12:00 hours at the office of
the Chief Procurement & Contracts Manager.

1.5 Eligibility: The bidder shall be a registered contractor with Pakistan
Engineering Council in category C-A (No Limit) and shall have a valid
affidavit of non-blacklisting from the relevant authority.

1.6 The bidder must have an average annual turnover of Rs. 2,500,000,000
(Rupees Two Billion Five Hundred Million only) over the last five audited
financial years.

1.7 The bidder must have completed at least 3 similar nature works of value
not less than Rs. 120,000,000 each during the last ten years.

1.8 The Contractor shall be responsible for obtaining all statutory approvals
and permits from the concerned authorities at its own cost and risk.

1.9 The contract shall be on turnkey basis and the Contractor shall be
responsible for the design of the extension works, except the 132kV bay
protection drawings which are provided by the Employer.

1.10 Price shall be firm and fixed. No price adjustment shall be allowed.

1.11 The Contractor's liability under this Contract shall be without limitation
of amount.

1.12 Liquidated damages for delay shall apply at the rate of 0.05 percent of
the Contract Price per day, subject to a maximum of 10 percent of the Contract
Price.

1.13 The Employer shall retain 5 percent of each running bill as retention
money, which shall be released on issue of the certificate of substantial
completion.

1.14 Performance security equivalent to 5 percent of the Contract Price shall
be furnished by the Contractor prior to the execution of the Contract.

1.15 The Contractor shall submit an advance payment of 15 percent of the
Contract Price on commencement, which shall be recovered from running bills.

1.16 All disputes arising out of this Contract shall be referred to arbitration
in accordance with the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of
Commerce, seat of arbitration being Singapore.

1.17 The Contractor shall bear all taxes, duties, income tax, sales tax and
withholding tax levied or imposed on the Contract Price, and shall provide
evidence of payment.

1.18 Each Bid shall be accompanied by a duly filled in Form of Tender, the
Schedules of Prices, and a power of attorney in favour of the person signing
the bid on behalf of the bidder.

1.19 The bidder shall furnish an undertaking on a stamp paper of Rs. 100 in
accordance with Form A-7.

1.20 Insurance: The Contractor shall arrange workmen's compensation, third
party liability and contractor's all risks insurance for an amount of not less
than the Contract Price.

1.21 The Contract shall be subject to the General Conditions of Contract of
the Pakistan Engineering Council, and the Special Conditions of Contract given
in these bidding documents. FIDIC conditions of contract for construction
(EPC/Turnkey) shall apply where not superseded.

1.22 The Employer reserves the right to terminate the Contract for convenience
on 30 days written notice, in which case the Contractor shall be paid for the
work executed and for reasonable demobilisation costs.

1.23 The Contractor shall provide a defect liability period of 12 months from
the date of substantial completion.

1.24 Bids shall remain valid for 120 days from the date of opening.

1.25 Clarifications, if any, shall be submitted in writing not later than
10-Nov-2026 and shall be communicated to all prospective bidders.
