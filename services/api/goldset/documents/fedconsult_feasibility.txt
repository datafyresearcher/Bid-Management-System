MINISTRY OF ENERGY (POWER DIVISION)
FEDERAL GOVERNMENT OF PAKISTAN

TENDER FOR HIRING OF CONSULTANCY SERVICES FOR FEASIBILITY STUDY AND
DETAILED DESIGN OF A HYBRID RENEWABLE POWER PLANT

REF: ME(Power)/CONS/2026/31
METHOD: Single Stage — Two Envelope
EVALUATION: Quality and Cost Based Selection

The Consultant shall prepare a feasibility study and detailed design for a
hybrid solar and wind power plant.

The last date of submission of proposals is 12-12-2026 at 1500 hours.
Proposals will be opened on 13-12-2026 at 1600 hours.

The Consulting firm must have completed at least 5 feasibility studies of
renewable power plants of at least 100 MW each during the last 10 years.

The firm must have at least 15 professionals on its roll including 5
energy economists.

The firm shall submit a proposal along with a Form of Technical Proposal, a
Form of Financial Proposal and a Power of Attorney.

The contract shall be on the basis of deliverables and time schedule.

Payments shall be made against acceptance of deliverables.

The Consultant shall be responsible for confidentiality of the data.

The firm shall be eligible to participate.

No performance security is required for this consultancy assignment.

The Consultant shall not be paid for the preparation of the proposal.

The final report shall be submitted in English and Urdu.

The contract shall be governed by the laws of Pakistan and the courts of
Islamabad shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
