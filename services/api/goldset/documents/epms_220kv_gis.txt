A.2 The cost of bidding documents is Rs. 45,000/- (Rupees Forty Five Thousand
only) which shall be paid by cash or pay order before the bidding documents are

A.3 The Contract Price is firm and fixed. No price adjustment or escalation
shall be allowed for any reason whatsoever.

A.4 Retention money shall be 10 percent of the running bill, released on

Contract Price in the form of an unconditional Bank Guarantee valid until

execution of the Contract against an advance payment guarantee.

B.5 The Bidder shall submit a duly filled in Form of Tender, a Schedule of
